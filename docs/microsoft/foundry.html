Microsoft Foundry: where the pieces fit

Microsoft Foundry is Microsoft's platform for building AI agents, models and apps. It brings models, agents, tools and the controls to run them under one management grouping, so the harness from step 2 has everything it needs around it.

Checked 31 Oct 2026New Foundry portal

The capability map

Agents sit in the middle. They run on models, act through tools and draw on knowledge. Guardrails check what goes in and out, evaluation shows how well it works, and the operate layer looks after everything across projects.

Where to find it in the portal

Portal sectionWhat you do there
DiscoverThe model catalog and model benchmarks.
BuildAgents, models, playgrounds, evaluations and fine-tuning in your project.
OperateCompliance, fleet health, tracing and assets across all your projects.
ManageQuota, AI Gateway, and resource and project details, including who has access.

Two kinds of model in the catalog

Models sold by Azure are hosted and supported by Microsoft. Models from partners and the community are supported by their providers, and they make up most of the catalog. Usually you deploy a model to your Foundry resource first, and every project on that resource can share the deployment. An instant-access preview, in one region so far, lets you call some models by name without a deployment.
