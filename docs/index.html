How we keep it accurate

Every statement has a source

Facts come from Microsoft's own docs and announcements, listed at the bottom of each page with the date we checked them.

What we saw is labelled

Anything we observed in our own Azure subscription is marked We saw this, with the date, because portals change and your results may differ.

Previews get re-checked

Preview features, prices and regions are checked again before each episode is published, and the page date moves with them.

Our diagrams are simplified

Diagrams are our drawings, not Microsoft's. Where we simplify, the caption says so.
